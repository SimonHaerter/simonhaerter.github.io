SIMON HAERTER

Simon Haerter is a Swiss graphic designer working across branding, identity, and campaigns.

Drawing inspiration from Swiss-style design and brutalist architecture, his practice combines clear visual language, boldness and motion with a deeply conceptual approach.

His process often involves breaking complex topics down to their fundamentals – and using those findings to build true-to-nature visual systems.
